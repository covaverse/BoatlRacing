## Boat audio: MetaSound + Sound Cues (2026-10-08, latest)

- The old per-component audio (7 AudioComponents, ~25 Audio variables, `EnsureLoop`) is gone. `EngineAudio` now plays **`MS_LongtailEngine`**, built by `Tools/create_boat_audio_assets.py` through the MetaSound builder API. It has six looping Wave Players: 4 engine RPM bands, exhaust and water. Each player gets a `<Layer>Gain`/`<Layer>Pitch` input multiplied by mix levels (`Master` 0.4, `EngineLevel`, `ExhaustLevel`, `WaterLevel`), and each layer's wave is a MetaSound input. Start, pops, hits and canal ambience are Sound Cues (`SC_*`).
- **Sound profiles:** `MS_SportCarEngine` is active. It uses a V8 Maserati GranTurismo S cut into 4 bands and pitched to the shared 1500/2400/3600/5600 references, plus a Ferrari 250 GTO V12 exhaust, both CC BY 4.0. `MS_LongtailEngine` is the alternative. Swap profiles via the EngineAudio component Sound. The canal map's Pawn carries an explicit override: `reset_properties` on that component reset Sound to None rather than to the Blueprint value. The 3 other test maps were not re-checked.
- `UpdateEngineAudio` derives a geared **sound RPM** from speed (`GearSpeedKmh` 15: climbs `GearLowRPM`->`GearHighRPM` per band, uses real RPM below 3 km/h). It smooths sound RPM and throttle with `FInterpTo` (`SoundBlendSpeed` 6), then sends 12 float parameters per frame. It restarts the MetaSound if it stopped. Lift-off pops use the `PopArmed` latch.
- Verified by recording UE's real output (`AudioMixerLibrary` via Python remote execution). Full throttle is -19.5 dBFS RMS with peak 0.38 and no clipping. The steady-speed pitch matches the formula (67 Hz predicted, 68 Hz measured). The pop fires once.
- **Drive test:** an in-editor per-frame autopilot drove 1820 of 1850 m at 110 km/h, staying within 0.9 m of the centreline with 0 bank hits and at most 3 degrees of steering. PIE was stopped externally 90 m before the finish. Bug: after a full-speed bank hit the hull pins to the wall, moving 9-20 km/h while `ForwardSpeedKmh` still reads 94-107.
- Real recordings and the two required CC-BY credits are listed in `SourceAssets/Audio/Real/CREDITS.md`. The guide is [audio guide](Guides/AUDIO_GUIDE.md).
- Gotchas:
  - Never compile during PIE: it re-instances the Pawn, and the foam MIDs go None every frame.
  - Never type into the editor Cmd box via Slate. Use Python remote execution. See memory.
  - Adding Blueprint variables while a map is open saves 0 overrides on its placed Pawn.

## Handling, trim and wake pass (2026-10-02, later still)

- **Hull lift in turns is fixed.** EventGraph lateral drag was built in hull space and transformed by the full hull transform, so roll turned part of a very large side force into lift; the hull climbed from 374 to 404 cm against a 400 cm water plane. Both `AddForce` sites now pass through `BreakVector -> MakeVector(X, Y, 0)`. Hull now holds 379.2-379.3 cm through a full-lock turn with roll steady at 3.48 degrees.
- Turning circle: steering 18 -> **38** degrees and `lateralDrag` 1200 -> **2400** take the radius from 223 m to **109 m**. 45/3200 reached 83 m but made hull roll ring, so it was not kept.
- Bow lift and bank are now **visual only**, in a new `ApplyVisualTrim` function on the `BoatVisual` child actor: -5.7 degrees bow-up from rest decaying to 0 by 75 km/h, and a roll that cancels the physical outward lean and banks into the turn instead (net -6 to -7.5 degrees). Physics torque was tried first and reverted — at 150M the hull rocked and overshot bow-down.
- Rear pontoon radius 55 -> **53**: resting pitch -1.65 -> -4.90 degrees and the propeller is finally wet at rest. `bCenterPontoonsOnCOM` is true, so moving engine mass aft does **not** change trim; the pontoons follow the COM. 53 leaves the rear spheres at 101% submersion with no reserve — recheck on the high-wave map.
- Wake impulses moved to (-240, +35, -20) and (250, -35, -20) so the stern half is no longer over flat water; speed/power terms raised (0.10 -> 0.22, 0.06 -> 0.13) and bow radius 55 -> 70, leaving the at-rest constants alone. Propeller spray enlarged to a rooster tail.
- FX no longer pop out of existence: `SetVisibility` on bow splash, prop spray and exhaust hid the whole component on the same condition that gates spawn rate, so lifting the propeller deleted every airborne droplet. All three are now always visible and controlled by spawn rate alone, so particles fade out naturally. Rates raised: prop spray x12 -> x26, bow splash 130 -> 260 per side, prop wash 4+24a -> 10+60a. No particle budget measured.
- **Trim curve corrected against published planing data.** `ApplyVisualTrim` had maximum bow-up at zero speed decaying to flat by 60 km/h, which is backwards. Planing hulls sit near level at rest, rear up through the hump, then settle at 3-4 degrees bow up, which is also the resistance minimum. The visual now targets a world pitch of `-(3.5*clamp(spd/40) + 8*clamp(1-|spd-28|/26))` and subtracts the hull's own pitch, so the total is exact: measured -0.01 at rest, -9.74 at 29 km/h, and a locked -3.5 from 62 to 110 km/h.
- Wake angle checked rather than changed: Kelvin's 19.47 degrees only holds below Fr 0.5, which for this 6.20 m hull is **14 km/h**; above that it narrows as 1/Fr to about 2.5 degrees at 110 km/h. The narrow band hugging the hull at racing speed is therefore correct and should not be "widened".
- 110 km/h and the 200 kg engine are defensible: tourist longtails run 15-30 km/h on 25-40 HP, Thai racing longtails exceed 200 km/h on 400+ HP automotive conversions. Sources and the full table are in [realism references](Work/2026-10-02-realism-references.md).
- Spray mist now animates: `SubUVAnimation` V2 module on both emitters with Random Start Frame, so each puff plays the 64-frame flipbook over its lifetime instead of freezing on frame 0. True CS2-style volumetric smoke is possible — `NiagaraFluids` Grid3D gas and `HeterogeneousVolumes`/`SparseVolumeTexture` are all installed — but `NiagaraFluids` is **not enabled** and a moving 3D grid is a cost class above the 2D ripple solver already running, so it was left as a decision. See [realism references](Work/2026-10-02-realism-references.md).
- Spray is now two layers and needed **no downloaded assets**: droplets (`NS_LongtailSideSpray`, 1.5-5 cm, gravity -1900, falls fast) plus a billowing haze (`NS_LongtailSprayMist` + `M_LongtailSprayMist`, built on the engine's `T_SmokeSubUV_8X8` flipbook, alpha-masked, 70-185 cm, drag 2.2). Opacity must come from the flipbook's alpha channel — using R renders hard-edged quads.
- Tall side spray added: `NS_LongtailSideSpray` (duplicated from the bow splash, cones turned upward, 180 cm spawn sphere), spawned at runtime by new `SetupSideSpray` / `UpdateSideSpray` functions and a `SideSprayFX` variable, because no tool can add a Blueprint component. Droplets are 1.5-5 cm at `300 + 1400a` per second with gravity -1900, drag 0.08 and 0.30-0.85 s life, so it reads as mist that falls like water rather than floating blobs.
- **Stern pivot failed twice and is reverted** — relocating the lateral drag fore of the centre of mass capsized the boat (roll -51, then 63 to 148 degrees). It needs a separate bounded yaw moment, not a moved drag force.
- Rear pontoon 53 is a trade-off: turn radius 155 m -> 17 m and the propeller finally wet at rest, but roll in a hard turn goes 8.5 -> 22.3 degrees. High-wave map still not run.
- **Open:** the requested stern pivot was not implemented — lateral drag uses `AddForce`, which acts at the centre of mass and adds no yaw, and the wanted direction was not resolved. `ApplyLaunchPitch` is now redundant and still running. See [handling pass](Work/2026-10-02-handling-pass.md).

- The fluid simulation's hard rectangular border is **fixed**: `M_WaterBoatContact` now fades the whole fluid contribution out over the last few percent of the grid, using the sim's own `Fluidsim_WorldPosition_to_UV` and a `BlendMaterialAttributes`. No Blueprint change was needed — `MPC_Water` already carries `SimLocation` and `FluidSimSize`. **Still open:** the boat sits at a constant ~0.377 of the way to the grid edge with the grid centred about 31 m ahead of it, so only ~10 m of domain remains behind the hull for the wake; the fade band had to be squeezed to 0.452-0.499 to avoid erasing it.
