# Water Physics Prototype

The gameplay Pawn `/Game/Boats/ThaiLongtail/Blueprints/BP_LongtailPhysicsPawn` uses a low-poly convex Chaos collision hull. Its 98-part visual child does not drive collision. Water Buoyancy pontoons are at local X +/-240 cm, Y +/-45 cm, Z 5 cm: two forward radii are 60 cm and two aft radii are 55 cm. The smaller aft support lets the engine-heavy stern settle lower. The flat test Ocean is at Z 400 cm with waves disabled.

`LoadHandlingProfile` runs before the mass and COM setters at BeginPlay. The current `/Game/Boats/ThaiLongtail/Handling/DA_ThaiLongtail_Handling` specifies 250 kg hull centered at X=-20 cm and 200 kg engine centered at X=+270 cm. Total mass is 450 kg; the intended longitudinal center of mass is `(250*-20 + 200*270)/450 = +108.89 cm` aft, **between** hull and engine centers, not at the engine. The tapered convex collider's estimated volume centroid is X=+75.78 cm, so `SetCenterOfMass` receives an X offset of about +33.11 cm. Desired COM Z=-20 cm means an offset of -25 cm from the collider centroid Z=+5 cm. A fresh Simulate BodyInstance readback gave `massInKgOverride=450` and `cOMNudge=(33.111,0,-25)`. These are game-tuning estimates, not weighed real-world specifications. Blueprint defaults are the nonzero fallback if the profile is absent or invalid; missing/invalid-profile PIE cases still need an explicit regression test.

Thrust is not applied at the measured submerged propeller position: that aft-and-low application point created an unwanted pitch moment. The force is projected onto world XY. Its application X/Y come from transforming boat-space `(240,0,-20)` cm, while application Z is read from the runtime hull COM in world space. Thus direct propulsion torque about world X/Y is zero, while the aft lever arm retains yaw leverage. A tilted hull still responds through buoyancy/inertia; this is a gameplay approximation rather than a full propeller/water interaction. Only hull pontoons supply buoyancy. Positive thrust scales with steering, trim cosine, clutch/shaft drive, propeller immersion, and a soft governor. The current profile has `DriveForce=1,200,000`, `QuadraticDrag=0.015`, `LateralDrag=1200`, governor start/target 105/110 km/h, cruise scale 0.45, and overspeed fade 6 km/h. `ForwardSpeedKmh` uses horizontal hull velocity only; no rigid-body velocity clamp is used. Both lateral-force graph paths now read `GetLateralDrag * -1`; the current profile therefore preserves the previous 1200 tuning while allowing per-boat adjustment.

`UpdateWaterPropState` now calls native `SampleWaveSurface` with `IncludeWaves` at the actual propeller hub. It rejects excluded/uncovered/non-finite samples; no valid water means zero immersion and drive. The aft-pontoon average was removed. Target hub depth remains about 4 cm for W/S; immersion rises from 0 to 1 over 4 cm. Automatic trim is capped at -40 degrees. Visual pivot-to-hub span remains 246 cm, root X=300 and hub X=546 cm. Hub contact is a blade proxy, not a per-blade simulation. `SetHullPontoonRadii` applies profile front/front/rear/rear radii after initialization without recalculating sprung-mass positions. Water's next Update copies these into async physics. A live temporary 59/54 profile read actual component radii 59/59/54/54; restored 60/55 and saved.

Coast steering uses `CalculateTailWaterForce`: project the tail side normal onto XY, measure water-relative crossflow at the COM-height application point, apply opposite quadratic drag. Wet factor is clamped immersion times `clamp((-TrimDegrees-3)/7,0,1)`; default coefficient 0.15, force bound 150,000 Unreal units. Application X/Y come from the actual hub and Z from runtime COM. This retains yaw leverage without vertical support or direct pitch/roll torque. Ocean spline metadata initially caused low-speed yaw despite river forces being disabled. `ResolvePhysicalCurrent` now returns current only for a River with `bApplyRiverForces=true`, matching Water force eligibility. It runs before velocity validity checks so ignored Ocean metadata cannot reject a good height sample. Overflowing relative subtraction/dot returns zero; quadratic force saturates before squaring. Four native tests passed after two reproduced failures. Live new-DLL Ocean sampling returned zero current. At rest with an immersed/steered tail speed settled near zero and yaw remained about -0.00027 degrees. Immersed coast right/left yaw reached about +13.3/-12.6 degrees; raised-tail coast held about +0.33957 degrees. These batched wall-time observations demonstrate regression behavior, not timed turn-radius or inertia acceptance.

`ApplyLaunchPitch` uses a one-shot envelope on engaged, wet, positive launch below 40 km/h, with negative torque about the hull's right axis. The profile currently provides 12,000,000 torque units for 0.8 s. Release/Space rearms the pulse; reverse, dry prop, and disengaged clutch cannot trigger it. It now cancels the envelope if water/drive eligibility is lost, instead of pausing airborne and resuming on reentry. Torque scales from full at -3 degrees pitch to zero at -7 degrees bow-up, without locking pitch. The pulse has triggered in PIE, but measured bow-rise magnitude, decay timing, and dry/clutch/reverse suppression still need controlled acceptance tests. Engine-only vibration is gated to this launch pulse and to loaded W operation above 108 km/h; it is not a hull force.

Historical low-speed baseline, **before the current handling profile and enlarged Ocean course**: at a 270/180 kg split, settled flat-water idle pitch was -1.605 degrees. W at 0.4 input reached 342 cm/s with pitch -1.605 degrees, trim -8.91 degrees, and hub Z=396.00 cm under a 400 cm surface. S at -0.4 reached 342 cm/s in reverse with similar pitch and trim. `W+Space` yielded EngineRPM 6500, ShaftRPM 0, DriveThrust 0, ClutchEngagement 0, and vibration envelope 0. A side-view PIE capture showed the stern lower than the bow and the long shaft shallow. Negative pitch means the bow is up. These historical snapshots are not timed acceleration or current top-speed acceptance evidence; an old prolonged W run left the former finite Water Body, and its freefall speed values were discarded.

The flat course Ocean spans roughly 1.5 km forward. A historical bounded full-W sample reached 110.10 km/h while wet with vibration envelope 1; 110 is a target, not an absolute cap. Small/High maps use separate Gerstner assets. Small idle after fixing a stale 48 cm rear override sampled pitch -1.4 to -2.2 degrees without runaway sink. High idle sampled -9 to -2.9 degrees and substantial heave; it remains a stress map. After native hub-query integration, small-wave 0.2-throttle samples returned water Z 361.94-395.95 cm, speed 10.97-16.33 km/h, pitch -4.55 to +2.59 degrees, with zero Ocean current. These are snapshots, not full wave/contact acceptance. A moving viewport capture showed the low-speed assembly and surrounding waves; it does not prove absence of water intrusion through every crest. No moving dry-hull render mask exists yet. The model still omits planing lift distribution, cavitation, ventilation, water entrainment, fuel, precise inertia, damage and capsize recovery. Finish physical/contact and visual-boundary tests independently; do not hide real sinking with a render mask.
